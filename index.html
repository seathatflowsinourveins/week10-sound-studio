How to save in the p5 editor
  1. Get the lesson ZIP and unzip it.
  2. Open p5 in a full browser tab and create a new sketch.
  3. Replace sketch.js and index.html with the supplied files. Upload sound-0.wav through sound-4.wav.
  4. Run, click the sound button, then Save. Paste your saved link into Worksheet here.
